I'm the unofficial camera person for our practice clips. Today Dain asked, "Why don't you ever get in the shot?" and then took my phone and pressed record on me.

Turns out standing in front of a lens while a friend watches is harder than any choreo. The first three takes, my arms forgot how to be arms. Take four, I did the chorus and ended with a pose so serious Sera said it looked like a bank robbery.

We kept the bank-robbery take. It's actually very good. There's something about not knowing what to do with your hands that is just honest.

I decided I'll practice being in front of the camera for ten minutes every night, same as stretching. Nobody told me the camera would be the hard part. Anyway, here's the evidence that I survived it.
