Quick errand: buy new practice socks at the shop near the station. Easy. Four minutes, according to everyone but me.

I followed the map app with total confidence for ten minutes in the exact wrong direction. A very kind ahjumma at a fruit stand pointed behind me and said, "Station is that way. Many people are lost here." I decided to take that as a compliment to the neighborhood.

Found the socks eventually, and a bonus bakery. I bought a bread called "milk cream," which tasted like a hug.

On the walk back I filmed the whole sidewalk in one long take, narrating my path like a nature documentary. "Here we see the trainee, lost again." It's going to be a good clip.

Practice tonight was sharp anyway. Strange how getting lost wakes up my legs.
