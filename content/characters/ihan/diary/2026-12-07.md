Couldn't sleep. Around two I gave up and took the stairs to the roof in my socks.

Cold air, a quiet city, one red light blinking on a far tower. I started counting the eight of us like a roll call. Dohyun, Minjae, Sunwoo, Eunchan, Hajun, Rui, Jihu, me. Then I stopped, because I didn't like what the counting was for.

I thought about the room downstairs, Eunchan's slow breathing, and how much I'd like all of us in the same picture six months from now.

I stayed until my toes hurt. Back in bed I was still awake, but less alone in it.
