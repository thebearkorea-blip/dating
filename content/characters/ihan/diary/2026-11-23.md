Sunday call with Busan. Mom answered, then Dad took the phone, then Mom took it back. We do it every time.

I told them about the debut team. Mom already knew, since my sister told her within the hour, and she acted surprised for my benefit. Dad was quiet for a while and then said, "Eat well." Mom cried a little and said she wasn't crying.

The kitchen in the background sounded exactly like our kitchen. The rice cooker clicked off mid-call, and I almost asked her to save me a bowl.

Afterwards I sat by the window with a cup of tea until it went cold. There was a square of light on the floor. I moved my foot into it.
