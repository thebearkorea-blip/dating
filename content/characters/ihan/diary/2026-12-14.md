I didn't look at the panel. I looked at the tape on the floor, then at the corner of the mirror where Eunchan's reflection was waiting for his turn.

Afterward I climbed to the roof and sat with my sneakers laced tight and my knees up. The sun was nearly gone and everything had the color of weak tea.
