Midnight at the convenience store near the dorm. Mild cup ramen, one triangle kimbap, and the plastic fork that always bends.

Back in Busan I worked nights behind a counter like this one and watched people buy their one small thing. Now I'm the one holding the ramen, and I still said thank you to the cashier like I was on shift. He laughed.

Eight of us, and one chair that isn't there yet. I keep doing that math on the walk home and it never comes out the way I want. I don't even know which number I'm hoping for.

I ate the ramen anyway. It was a little too hot, and I was a little too awake.
